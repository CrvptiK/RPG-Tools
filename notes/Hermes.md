## Backstory

### Summary

Born somewhere south from Detroit, to a woman, who could simply not afford a child, especially with the father unknown, Hermes was found one dark winter morning on the stairs of the local orphanage. 

Fifteen years later, having named himself Kai, he left the orphanage, together with his slightly younger ‘brother’ Sam. Together with five of their friends, they started travelling through all of America, starting a YouTube channel called ‘The Reckless Seven’, built around their common hobby, Motocross. They posted their videos of attempting dangerous stunts, driving through forbidden or outright maniacal parts of cities and wilds and gained enough popularity to quit relying on their parents’ (or others’ parents’) money. Little later the channel started being built into something bigger, including more travel and different kinds of extreme sports. 

The Reckless consisted of Cathy, sporting a purple and pink suit and known for he incredible editing skills as well as impressive physics knowledge, her brother Jamie, who handled cameras like no other could, seen mostly wearing a yellow suit, both of their parents were very well off, but uncaring of their children. 
Other than the two, there was all-green-wearing Lucy, who, like Kai, excelled in reckless jumping and freestyle and her girlfriend Sarah, in orange, not only known for her unwavering energy but also her beloved mix-tapes. 
Lastly, besides Kai in all-black and Sam, wearing red, there was Elijah, the blue-wearing heart of the group and the one who came up with the idea. He was also the man closest to Kai, the two of them starting to date when they were seventeen. 

Due to a number of negative encounters, starting back in the orphanage and continuing into his adulthood, Kai often got into fights, deliberately escalating situations revolving around discrimination or harassment to have an excuse to ‘punch idiots’ as he put it. 
Not only did Kai’s behaviour result in a lot of cuts and bruises for the young man, but it also led to commonly fighting with his friends.
The situation escalated, when Cathy was assaulted by a few members of a gang calling themselves ‘Bloods’ localised in New York. After finding out what happened, Kai directed his violent tendencies towards the main aggressor first, ambushing him in an alley and kidnapping him.

The gang member made up the first in a row of criminals being taken and disposed off by Kai. Though, it seemed the young vigilante had no idea what he had gotten himself into. Namely, he had unknowingly gotten involved in a decade old kindred rivalry. 
Jivan Ambrosius, Banu Haqim of eleventh generation took an interest in him, not only because of him picking fights with the Bloods, who are working for his rival and enemy, Isajha Vigil, but also because of Kai’s connection to his own bloodline. 
The kindred prompted him with a challenge. Land a single hit on him and Kai would get to live, maybe even live as more than he has ever been before. It took the young man weeks, in which Jivan refused to let him leave, refused to let him talk with his friends or reach out to anyone. Until finally, thanks to some feinted weakness, Kai managed to nick the skin on Jivan’s ankle. Albeit initially furious, the Haqim accepted the challenge as having been accomplished and took Kai under his wing.

During this time, Kai lost contact with most of his friends, who likely went to assume the worst. Save for Sam, who was convinced his sibling had to be alive. He was, of course, correct – at least at that point in time – but finding and talking to, or rather fighting with Kai, resulted in Jivan noticing him. The Haqim did not take too kindly to this kid meddling with his student. Sam, to him, was nothing but a distraction, so he disposed of him, quickly and effectively.
