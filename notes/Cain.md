## Backstory

### Wretched and Divine

“Why do you always get into fights over nothing?”, Ryan pressed a piece of dirty cloth, drenched in the booze they had stolen a few days prior, to the wound onto Cain’s forehead. 
“Nothing?! They treated you like some-” 
“And? They treat everyone like that! Even you. And you don’t mind that…”, the brunette remarked quietly. He touched up the cloth again. This time, Cain flinched a little at the stinging pain. They sat in silence for a moment, Cain contemplating his friend’s words, while Ryan simply kept his eyes trained on the injury. 

“I’m sorry. I shouldn’t have- I just could not take them talking about you like that.” He looked at the sky, unable to even glance at his friend. Ryan looked at him sadly, putting down the cloth and bottle. “People will always talk, Cain. It’s not up to you what they say. All you can control are your own actions.” Blue, aggrieved eyes found brown, compassionate ones. Cain sighed in defeat. 
“You’re right. I’m sorry.” 

-

Barely a few days passed, before the same group of youths showed up at the friends’ den, demanding grub, water and bullets, in return for what they called ‘protection’. Protection from themselves that was. When Cain returned to Ryan nursing a broken, bloodied nose, the little bit of booze – pretty much all they had when it came to resources – taken, leaving them with nothing, the talk they have had was all but forgotten. 

The ‘Golden Vipers’ were nothing much but an upstart group of scoundrels, trying to establish themselves as some sort of ‘gang’. These people thought they could take everything they wanted, hurt everyone they did not care for. Cain never much cared for what they did. They were just another group of assholes in a world filled with nothing but the worst individuals.
He would have let them be. Let them continue their schemes and ignored their actions. But not when it came to his best friend. He would tear them to shreds, for what they had done to Ryan. 

-

“Guys, let’s do something fun!”, one of the Vipers chanted drunkenly, he held up a scrap gun. The implication of what he wanted to do was obvious. The thought of wasting bullets on something like this, was simply ridiculous. 
None of the drunkards noticed the black haired teen, who had made it right into their den. Cain was not entirely certain, how his mutation worked yet, but he supposed, if he could take the gun out of the fight, he might have a chance to win this. Suddenly an arm wrapped around his throat, pulling him back and off the ground. He stifled a surprised outcry, but that did not help his situation, as the guy behind him called out to his friends. 
“Looks like we got a lost mutt, looking for some scraps! Boys!” 

Cain could barely get enough air into his lungs to refrain from panicking. He needed an out. So, using this moment, before the rest of the bunch showed up, he let one of his arms fall, before bringing a fist to the older man’s head. Not having expected this, Cain was practically thrown to the ground, only barely landing on his feet. 
Now was not the time for revenge, he realised, he just needed to get away. Which proved difficult, as the Vipers spotted both him and their obviously injured leader. The pistol was raised and before Cain really knew what he was doing, the command was already given. 
Bang-
The gun fell to the dirt ground, just like the lifeless body of the shooter. 

Cain ran. As quickly as his legs would carry him. He just fled the scene. And when he arrived back at his den, Ryan was right there, waiting for Cain to explain himself. But before he could even begin reprimanding his friend for his actions, his eyes widened in terror, his jaw dropping in disbelief.
“What happened to you?! Whose blood is this- What-?!”, Ryan’s questions fell on deaf ears. Cain just looked at his friend, not quite understanding what had just gone down.

And then he caught a glimpse of himself in the cracked window of their little home. Crimson blood covered his face, painting a bizarre and frightening mosaic of gore. But worst of all was his hair, the black having shifted to a dark scarlet tone, almost as if it wanted to remind him of what he had done just now. Because he could wash off the blood on his hands, he could remove all traces of it from his skin. But his hair – he doubted it would ever return to the colour it had been before tonight. 

### Not A Human Being

Cain looked down at his friend, who was writhing in pain from his injury, which had gotten infected. He did not know what to do. They did not have any resources to pay a bonesaw or scrounge for any medicines, so it would come down to whether or not Ryan could fend off the fever. The redhead felt tears prickling in his eyes, the fear of loosing his best friend stinging like a fresh injury. He held Ryan’s hand, it felt blazing to the touch, making worry coil like a snake around his insides. There was no way he would let him die, no matter what it took. 

-

“Now, look who’s back! Feel bad about what you’ve done to Gem, so you’ve returned to let us kill you?”
Cain froze where he stood, slowly turning to look at the Golden Viper, who had spotted him and was now aiming a loaded pistol at his chest. He raised his hands in surrender, but the older boy was not risking him catching a glimpse of his eyes, “Don’t look this way! You’re one of those Puppeteer Mutants, don’t you dare pull that shit again!” 
The redhead obeyed, not moving. “I’m here to strike a deal, not to hurt anyone.”
“There is no way I’m letting you hurt any of us again, you sick bastard!”
“Please, I promise, I’m not here to cause trouble, just hear me out!” He turned his head just slightly, enough to cause the other to shoot at the ground, making Cain close his eyes in defeat. If this was how he died, so be it. At least he will have died trying to save his friend. 

“What do you have to offer me?!”, Brenn, leader of the Golden Vipers barked, seemingly appearing out of nowhere. “You’re that fucker, who keeps picking fights with my guys because of your stupid slave friend, aren’t you? What could you possibly give me, that would make me want to help you, of all people?” 
Cain bit down the anger boiling inside of him at the insult towards Ryan, reminding himself that he needed their help and he could not fuck this up. “I heard you stole medicine from a group of bonesaws. I- I need some of it. I’ll give you anything- please.” 
The two older guys shared a glance over Cain’s head. Then the young man regarded him coldly, considering his answer slowly, intentionally prolonging the moment of uncertainty and fear eating at Cain’s nerves. “Please.” His voice was thin, as he begged, getting to his knees. He would never have expected to find himself in this position, grovelling at the gang boss’ feet like a lost dog. But he would do whatever it took to keep Ryan save. 

“Anything, you say?”, Brenn sneered. He was looking down upon Cain. “You have nothing to your name. No bullets, no grub, no water, not even booze... 
But you can fight. You can work. Can ya not?” The redhead nodded faintly, not looking up. “I’ll give you the medicine you are asking for. But in return, you will belong to me. You will be property from this day onwards.”
There was a beat of silence. “Deal.” 
The gang leader grinned at that, gesturing for his lingering enforcers to move closer. A slave stopped next to Brenn, taking silent orders, before vanishing to retrieve the promised goods. The enforcers though moved closer to get a hold of Cain’s shoulders, forcing him up and locking him in place. He looked up in surprise, fear grasping at him, looking to the gang boss, silently searching for an explanation. 

“You know, normally I don’t tend to do this to my property, they know their place. But you- You’ve been a bit of a thorn in my side. So I want you to always have a reminder of where. you. stand.”
Struggling served no purpose, Cain soon found. Still, he attempted to break out of their hold over and over again. His fighting grew more desperate, as he realised what the gang leader was up to. Brenn was heating two pieces of carefully bent metal over an open flame. 
“Wait, you don’t have to do that, I’ll-”, he was cut off by his own scream as the glowing metal was jammed into his right thigh. The smell of burning flesh made his stomach turn. Searing pain made his vision go white. It took him an infinitely long moment to realise the metal had been withdrawn. He did feel the second symbol being burnt into his flesh though. When the two enforcers let go of him, Cain immediately toppled to the ground, hands hovering uselessly in the air over his blistering skin, as he writhed in pain. 

He barely took note of a box containing a tiny amount of pills and capsules, being put down next to him, before the Golden Vipers retreated, leaving him lying on the dirt ground in front of one of their camps. 

-

Ryan had recovered. After weeks of torturous waiting and worrying he was finally back on his feet. Cain could not be more relieved, even with everything else growing more difficult by the day. Brenn had a knack for sending Cain to do the dirtiest work, leaving the redhead’s hands covered in the same crimson shade it had been so many times by now, drawing his laboured breaths as he hauled materials and useless scrap for hours on end. The gang leader also had an uncanny talent for catching the younger completely off-guard. 

One evening when Cain was done working himself to the bone, Brenn had gotten a hold of him by his hair. The redhead had cut it afterwards to not give the other a chance at repeating the humiliating gesture. But in turn, Brenn had laughed, stating how easily manipulatable the younger had to be. It almost seemed like a game to him, doing whatever he wanted, without ever facing any consequence for it. With every passing day he grew crueller and Cain grew angrier.

Careful fingers wandered over the fresh scars on Cain’s hands and arms, before stopping and hovering over the burn marks. The redhead was lying leaned against Ryan’s chest, eyes closed, trying desperately to find it within himself to just rest.
“I’m sorry”, Ryan broke the silence after another long moment, during which his hands remained in the air, not touching the by now healed scar on Cain’s leg. “You did this to help me and now look at what that’s caused.” 
The younger turned around semi-successfully, looking up at Ryan with wide eyes. For a moment he thought to have misheard, but seeing his friend’s tears, he quickly scrambled to get to an upright position and properly face him. “Don’t apologise, please.” 
He pulled him close, their foreheads touching. “Don’t apologise for that. Ever again. You know, I’d do it again if I had to. I would carve out my own heart if it meant you would be safe. And none of that would be on you. It would be on me, it would be my choice. Don’t tell me it wouldn’t, because we both know you couldn’t stop me, could you? Don’t ever apologise for such a thing again, none of this was your fault, Ryan!” 
He wiped his tears carefully, prompting Ryan to finally nod in silent understanding. The older pulled him into a hug then, sniffling faintly. 

### Torn Apart

The sun touched down upon the horizon, dragging a murky veil of red over the sky above Ryan and Cain. The younger offered the can of grub he had managed to snatch from Brenn’s stash to his friend, who raised his brows curiously, “Where did you get that from?” 
“Where do you think?”, the redhead flashed him a smirk, that was met with a slightly amused but otherwise annoyed head shake. 
“You know he’ll figure out it was you.” 
Cain merely shrugged, opening the can himself, before holding it out teasingly once more. “How would he know?”  “No one else is stupid enough to take anything from him.” 
“Or brave enough”, he winked, as the other finally accepted the food, “Besides, we won’t be
around, once he figures it out.” This caused the older to pause, looking at Cain in a mix of confusion and worry. “Why’s that?” “I heard a rumour ‘bout something out in the zone. I looked into it a bit and I’m sure it’s to do with Eden.” “Cain, please-” “This time I’m serious!” 
“You said that last time as well! And the time before!”, Ryan crossed his arms over his chest, “This-“, he pointed towards Cain’s chest. He was referring to the scar drawn across it, caused by a sharp plate of rusted metal the redhead had needed to scramble over in an attempt to hide from zone ghouls. “Is the only thing you’ll be finding out there. One day you’ll run into something that you cannot avoid. Don’t you understand, I can’t stand the thought of you dying out alone in the zone!”
This was far from the first time they were talking about this. But maybe it was the first time the brunette was being this brutally honest. Cain looked down, eyes dragging over towards the horizon, where the zone lay in pitch black darkness. After a long moment, he sighed. 
“I know you worry for me. I know you’re scared. And so am I. I understand the risk of going out-” 
“And yet, you still choose to go out there!” 
“Because it is the only hope we have! Finding Eden… I know it is out there somewhere. And I know I will be the one to find it.” 
“What makes you so sure of that?” Ryan finally stopped staring at his friend, instead looking out over the Ark. Slowly, lights sprung up all across it, combating the dark. Silence stretched between them.
“I don’t know. But staying in here, working ourselves to the bone for a bastard like Brenn…” His eyes fell upon his leg, resting on the scar – it was so much worse than the one on his chest, speaking of the terror he knew laid hidden within the Ark’s borders. “I know sooner or later, that will kill us. And I would rather die out there, free from him, than trapped in here…” 
Once again, silence stretched between the two like the starless sky above their heads. 
“We could make our own Eden. Change this Ark for the better…”, Ryan’s eyes were locked on the specks of light beneath them. All Cain saw was the darkness in between. 
“This isn’t Eden. This is Hell.” 

One bullet. All he had. In the end, it was better than nothing. Loading the scrap pistol, before strapping it to his belt and closing his jacket to hide it, he looked out towards the zone. 
“I can’t believe we’re doing this”, Ryan once again counted over what little supplies the two owned, all gathered in the little satchel slung over his shoulder. “And you’re sure-” 
“We’ll be fine, Ry. I’ll get us to Eden safe and sound.” Still unconvinced, the brunette clutched the satchel nervously. “Hey”, Cain carefully gathered his hands between his own, “Trust me?” “Always.” 

“I told you all would be well”, Cain chimed lightly, taking off his jacket to fold it into a makeshift pillow. The two were huddled in one of the Ancients’ mechanical beasts. The redhead had explained to his friend how the Ancients had used these cars to travel around, powering them with a substance they dubbed gasoline, the closest thing he could compare it to being the booze they sometimes got their hands on in the Ark. Ryan had listened intently, unaware his friend had developed such an interest in the Ancients’ ways. “How long do you think it’ll take us to get to that place of yours?” 
Cain wiped a few drops on water from his lip, lowering the half-filled bottle, “One or two days from here, I would guess.” “Guess?” “Well, I haven’t been there before, have I?” He smiled teasingly.


Cain froze, as he stumbled across the body, Ryan all but running into him. He stared down at his find in utter horror. Only half a corpse, missing everything from the hip up, laid before him, the puddle of blood almost reaching to where he stood. Half-aware, he noted his friend stumbling backwards, valiantly trying not to throw up. The body could not be older than a few hours, Cain realised in horror, as he touched the blood. Whatever had done this was likely still around. 
Just as the thought crossed his mind, the faintest rustling reached his ear. Something was above him.
Throwing his arms up to shield his head and dropping down to evade the attack, the beast went down right with him, staying close on his tail. Albeit not managing to catch the redhead properly, which would have certainly doomed him to the very same unfortunate fate of the mutant, who had encountered this thing before them, teeth grazed across Cain’s right arm, tearing through skin and flesh easily. He rolled off, scrambling to his feet, just as the creature did as well. Its hollow, merciless gaze was locked on him, prompting the young mutant to anticipate its next move, dodging to the side just in time, the beast barrelling past him with frightening brute strength. 
The creature, with red, hate-filled orbs glaring at its prey, roared a frightening war cry. 
Suddenly Cain was pulled up by his good arm, Ryan at his side, gaze also trained on the beast. When it moved again, the two scattered. Cain hoped it would go after him. His arm was burning, as if set on fire, the skin around the wound bubbling, as if badly inflamed. If this was anything to go by, the bite of that thing was even more deadly than already anticipated. Panic squeezed his throat, as he fumbled for the gun on his hip. 
To Cain’s horror, the creature had turned its attention towards Ryan, closing in on the older mutant with horrifying momentum. The brunette had moved through an array of tightly grown trees, using the beast’s size against it. And yet, against this force of nature, smart thinking did not seem to be enough. Its teeth closed around Ryan’s leg with unyielding force. It was a miracle the limb was not immediately severed, as Ryan kicked it with his good leg, and scrambled away further, thankfully managing to free himself from its vice-like grip. 
Cain, desperate to help his friend, but knowing full well one bullet would not deter it, unless hit perfectly, picked up a rock from the ground, hurling it with every bit of strength he could muster. 
“Eyes on me, you ugly mutt!” 

The beast turned, its disfigured face almost split in two, rows of blood-covered teeth ready to tear Cain to shreds. Slowly, he drew back, careful to keep his eyes on the monster in front of him. 
“Come and get me”, he muttered under his breath.
The moment it was close enough to guarantee a good hit, its claws mere millimetres from Cain’s  face, he pulled the trigger. 
The gunshot tore through the air, immediately accompanied by the agonized roar of the creature, now blindly lashing out. Cain had shot it right in the eye, catching it off guard. 
Yet, he was not fast enough to draw back. Pain exploded behind his own eyes, as the beast’s claws slashed across his face and weapon arm. He staggered, hands coming up to cover a wound that was better left untouched. Red covered his vision, blurs of motion the only thing he could make out. Tears were quick to mix with the blood, burning in the wounds, as he desperately tried to just see something. 
When his vision finally cleared enough to make out the faintest outlines of his surroundings, he noted with a start, that the monster was gone. Panic still gripping his heart like a vice, Cain scrambled over to where Ryan had run. The brunette hardly kept on his feet, clutching his abdomen, where the beast had slashed him. 
“Fuck, that-” looked horrific. But it was nothing compared to Ryan’s leg. The limb had been mangled and torn into, the acidic saliva of the beast had burnt its way deep into the flesh, revealing chalk white bone, between discoloured bits of skin and bloody flesh. Cain almost choked on his tears at the sight. But they did not have any time to spare, if they wanted to survive. 
That thing could return any second.

It was the only thought keeping him going, as Cain willed himself forward, Ryan’s arm slung over his shoulder, dragging the taller mutant with him. His legs were shaking by now and his grip kept on slipping, causing them to stop every once in a while.
They had made it out of the forest, but the sand beneath their feet only made it harder to track further. The redhead refused to acknowledge his friend kept passing out for ever increasing amounts of time. The tears falling from his eyes stained the sand beneath him red, just like Ryan’s blood did. Another fact he refused to believe.
Only when his feet gave out under him, did he pause, desperately trying to catch his breath. Ryan was looking at him, eyes darkened by agony. He gritted his teeth, trying to speak, blood bubbling up, staining his teeth dark red. 
“Don’t talk! We’re almost back, we almost made it!” A desperate lie. They still were a day’s walk away. And that would be without Cain carrying his friend. “You’ll be fine. Everything will be fine!”
Thin words came over Ryan’s lips, almost like a whisper, pain choking him. “I’m sorry…”
“No, no, no, you’ll be alright, I-”, he should be the one apologising. He had caused this. It was his fault. “I got you, please, please, don’t-” Ryan’s hand, holding onto Cain’s a moment earlier, slowly went slack. A small piece of metal, covered in red, was all that remained in Cain’s otherwise empty palm.
*Please don’t leave me. Please don’t go.*
Either way the sentence ended, Ryan did not get to hear it. He was gone.

“Wake up! Please, Ryan. Please wake up! Wake up! Wake up!” 

It was futile. For all the power he had to force another to move – to do whatever he willed them to, there was no way to force the dead to wake. And Ryan was just that. Dead.
And Cain – Cain *screamed*.

### Fire That Remains

Fury was a horrid thing. It started as a kindling little flame, springing to life deep inside one’s heart. And then it grew, ate up everything inside you, until that growing flame finally consumed your every waking thought. It’s what happened to Cain that evening. Ryan was gone. He was gone and Cain had never felt worse before. It had been his fault. He had suggested that search for Eden, had convinced him to run together. To run from Brenn and from every bit of agony awaiting them on any given day inside the Ark. To run from the safety and protection their home offered. To run, only to come face to face with death. And instead of finding his own death, Ryan had lost his life. He had paid for Cain’s stupidity. Ryan was gone and Cain had lost everything.

His fingers were bleeding from the work forced upon him, sawing, splitting and stripping wood, both for the fire and the repairs Brenn’s guys had been working on for days now. It had not been the first time, nor would he have expected it to be the last. The sun was setting, the sky growing ever darker by the minute, when Brenn and his enforcers returned. The group of workers, all of them were considered to be the same thing, property, looked up, taking this as their cue to finally be able to leave. 

While they all dispersed quickly, Cain finished hauling the last log into place. When he turned to leave, there were only a handful of people still around. None of them even so much as glanced his way, when Brenn called his name. The redhead turned his head, but otherwise refused to react. He did not feel like playing the gang leader’s games tonight. His entire body was screaming at him to just walk away and rest, if only for a few hours. The last thing he wanted was to suffer through the older man’s cruel machinations once again. 

He let out the tiniest sigh, as he squeezed his eyes shut. He could do this. Whatever the head of the Vipers had in store for him today, Cain could take it. 
“I need this delivered to Adventure Land. Zelena’s guys probably expected it to arrive hours ago. Better not make them wait any longer, huh?”, the brunette’s sadistic tone and wretched smile effortlessly pushed Cain closer towards the edge. He threw a cautionary glance towards the crates. Four of equal size. Depending on their weight, he could maybe carry two at a time. If at all. “That’s on the other end of the Ark, Brenn. Can’t this wait until tomorrow?” 
The gang boss made a show of considering his words, seemingly weighing the options in his mind, before that grin returned to his features, twisting into a mockery of empathy. “I’m sorry, but I don’t think it can. Now, you better hurry along.” 

Cain in turn did not move an inch. Instead, unnatural eyes remained trained upon the older mutant. After a few agonising seconds Brenn’s smile slowly died on his lips. 
“Get a fucking move on.” When he did not receive any reaction, he snapped, “I gave you a fucking order. Need I remind you of your fucking place?!” 
Still refusing any sort of answer or reaction, Cain watched him closely, anticipating his next move. When Brenn snapped his fingers, his enforcers immediately raising to their feet and rushed over from where they had been sitting at the fire, the younger mutant finally raised his voice.
“What, too much of a coward to handle this yourself? You need your mutts to keep your people in line, huh?”, he took a step closer, feeling the heat radiating off of Brenn. The man was fuming. “Great boss that you are. Someone refuses your order and all you do is call a beating, instead of raising a hand yourself. Why is that, hm? I’m done listening to a coward such as yourself. Find someone else to deliver your fucking shit.”

He really should have seen the punch coming. But even with pain flaring up, his vision briefly flickering white, upon the breaking of his nose, he could not help the grin tugging at his lip. Cain’s retaliation was swift and merciless, his left fist managing to land right below Brenn’s ribs, only to grab him by the hair and use all the momentum to break his face on the redhead’s knee. 
The gang leader screamed in a mix of pain and rage. His enforcers stepped closer, but he commanded them to stand back. “Back off! I’ll break that bastard myself!”
The grin on the younger’s face, now painted red by his own blood only grew wider. 

How right it had felt in that moment, making Brenn eat his words. He did not give him time to recover, closing the distance between them quickly and twisting his arm behind his back, before swiping away his feet. The boss toppled to the ground, Cain immediately upon him, landing strike after strike after strike. 
Until Brenn twisted around, winding out from under him and throwing him off. Scrambling to his feet, the brunette panted heavily, left eye swollen shut, spitting out blood. As well as a tooth. A cruel chuckle escaped Cain at the sight. 
Brenn was upon him within the next second, having picked up a scrap axe, which had previously been used by a slave working under him. The smile on Cain’s lips faltered slightly, but he did not back down. 

Instead, when the strike came, he dodged to the side in the very last second, getting a hold of the weapon and throwing the boss to the ground once again, tossing the axe away. The short moment of distraction was enough though for Brenn to push back to his feet and rush the younger mutant. Cain scrambled, before his foot caught on an edge and his back hit the ground painfully. Hands closed around his throat, cutting off his air. Heat was biting at his skin, prickling dangerously right beside him. He glanced towards the fire, where the Vipers’ enforcers had sat only a few moments earlier. 

Panic was a driving force. And so was anger. Pure unbridled hatred for the man in front of him caused Cain to not even register the agonising stinging and the smell of burning skin, as he blindly reached for a still glowing piece of charcoal and brought it up against Brenn’s already bloody eye. He did not let him pull away, instead following his frantic retreat and managing to wrestle him into the ground again, kneeling over his writhing form to keep the burning log in place. 

When Brenn finally lost consciousness, Cain let off, throwing the coal back towards the fire pit and rising to his feet. His breathing was shallow. He could not feel his left hand, except for the dull throbbing agony washing over him in waves. But it did not matter. Nothing mattered right now. The superficial pain was nothing compared to the agony that Ryan’s absence caused.
The three enforcers looked between their boss and the man, who had just beat him. The man, who walked away then, not seeming to fear being followed. Not seeming to fear death itself. 

*If you wish to, join me. With me you will not be doing the grunt-work for anyone. You will never have to cower ever again.*
*I plan to make this Ark a better place. The best version of itself. And I will fight with all I have and all I am to make this dream our reality. If you share my dream and share my ambition, join me. We will build our own Eden, right here and now.*

Having spoken these words a mere few hours ago felt like venom on his tongue. Like setting foot upon a burning pyre. Like a noose winding tightly around his neck. The knife between his fingers was proof of his weakness. Or was it proof of strength? 
Either way, when he brought the glowing hot steel to the skin on his upper leg, he felt his words suffocating him. He glared at his tainted skin, at the mark of the Golden Vipers, a symbol of his faults, of his weakness, of his own cowering.

He tasted iron. It felt like blood filled his lungs, crawling up his throat, coating his tongue in deep dark crimson. Cain closed his eyes, breathing deep, before pressing the burning knife into and under his skin. The cloth between his teeth served its purpose in muffling his scream, before he could bite through the pain and continue dragging the knife down.

Suddenly desperate – it was not supposed to take this long! - he dropped the knife back into the flames, hardly noticing the fire licking at his fingers in the process. He wiped his tears, attempted to wipe at the blood and then drew the blade back up, this time pressing into and through the skin below the burn mark. 
In one swift – agonisingly slow – motion, he tore through his skin and flesh. The smell of burnt flesh hit him practically out of nowhere, making his stomach turn and his mind reel. And then, finally, he was done. His breath came in rugged, panicked heaves, tears and blood mixed on the dirt ground. He dropped the knife, falling back against the wall behind him. 


Cain could not help but wish for Ryan to be here. He would give up everything, would burn down the world, just to hear him say it would be alright. To feel Ryan’s arms around him, keeping him safe from the hell he found himself in. But he wasn’t. He was gone. And Cain found himself alone. His hands shook, maybe from the emotional shock, or maybe from the physical pain. He could not tell. But it did not matter. He glanced at his leg, at the mostly cauterised wound, where part of his skin was glaringly missing. 

When he realised he could still make out the faint trace of the letters ‘G’ and ‘V’, that were burnt into him, he barely managed to scramble aside, before his stomach forced up the little content it had. His mind was screaming at him. And by now his tears had turned into despaired sobbing.

### Who’s In Control

Cain shook his head in disbelief. Zelena was just not listening to him. She never listened. Instead she dropped onto her armchair, reaching over to fish for one of those damned bottles. 
“Zelena, this is not the time-”, he tried but was immediately interrupted by her audible sigh. “You’re such a fuckin’ buzz-kill. Sit your pretty ass down and take a swig, makes talking a lot easier.” 
She poured two glasses, putting one of them down on the table before him. “Trust me, c’mon!”
“We both know, how this ends…” She laughed, raising the glass. “Fucking relax, Cain!” 

Looking down at the dark amber liquid, uncertainty gnawed at his insides. His gaze flickered towards Zelena, his eyes finding hers, searching for some type of maliciousness, some telltale sign of her lying to him. In the end, he raised the glass and downed it. It burned his throat, drawing a cough from him. “What is in there?! That is-”, he had to bite down the rest of his sentence, to keep the thick alcohol down. He should have eaten, before coming here.

Putting down the glass heavily, he cleared his throat, “Can we talk now?!” His gaze burned into Zelena, who appeared completely unbothered. “Of course”, she chimed, pushing herself up and strutting over, the liquid in her glass sluggishly twirling around, “Let’s talk.”
