## Backstory

### Red Exile

“The Ruby District, where the streets run red and terror consumes the minds of innocents. Innocents. Are there innocents? Is it not a sin already to be born into this blighted world? Why else would we be here, if not to repent for the sins of our forebearers? Or our own.
I am getting ahead of myself. Apologies. 
… 
“How I ended up here, you ask? I can tell you my story, of course, if you are truly keen on knowing?
… 
“Fine then. Settle down.

“Trust is such a fragile little thing, is it not? It is built upon sand. Built over the course of years. Taking blood, taking sweat, taking tears. 
… 
“This is relevant to my story. If you cannot bear my voice, you should not have inquired my tale. 
… 
“Well then, where did I leave off? 
Right, trust. Is it not funny how the tiniest crack in it will soon lead to it crumbling into dust? The church once trusted me. They adored my work. And yet, the crack remained. That tiny voice in their heads, telling them not to trust my kind. That insidious little thought creeping up over and over.
All it took were five little words.
**You left them for dead.**

*A smile. A chuckle. Then a cough. Blood hitting the dirty ground.*

“They believed him. Of course they did. Such a fragile thing, trust is. Cracking, crumbling, falling. It took all this work to get them to trust me. They loved sending me out to hunt. I loved being sent. The only pay I needed was blood. 
Scourged, or not, in the end, we all bleed red, is it not ironic? 
… 
“Red is a beautiful color, is it not? Crimson, Scarlet, and yes, Ruby.
… 
“I have my doubts. But I will not impose on what you deem the truth. 
… 
“Not much left to tell, I fear. When only two of seven return, questions are to be expected. His lies were hollow, but it hardly mattered. ‘It left them for dead’ becomes easy to believe when the one painted evil is already mistrusted. Is already half a monster. 
… 

*A laugh, ragged, almost demonic. Crimson-tinted teeth. Scarlet-covered hands. Ruby droplets continuing to paint the road dark red.*

“He walked away that day. Soon he will learn he should have run. 
Me? I do not get to walk away. No. No no no no no. They locked me in chains and threw me here, hoping the district might tear me apart. 
Soon, Ruby shall paint every street of Luyarnha, it shall stain the soil, flood the streets and cleanse this beautiful city of all its blights. 
This is only the beginning.”

### Simpler Times or Memory 1

“You have been scowling at this for almost an hour now… Do you wish to talk about your theories? Or would you prefer continuing to glare holes into the papers?” Caligo, back then names Asier leaned over to take a closer look at his sister’s work. Their research has come far, yet, it still had further to go.
Ione, now Elysia, glanced at him, before dropping her head briefly, getting up to her feet. 
“Show me some of your paintings, will you? I am growing tired of imaginary discoveries.” His twin gave him the faintest of smiles. 

“You know, I never quite realized the beauty of this city at night”, her fingers carefully trailed over the dried white paint, his rendition of the pale moon painting the background in faint but eternally pure light, the buildings touched by faint silver, standing silhouette against the skies. “I wish I could see the world through your eyes more often.” 
“That is what art is all about, is it not? To show you – everyone – what beauty lies hidden within…” 

Caligo could not tell, could not fathom, why this exact memory had not been swept away, was not diluting and fading, like all the other phantoms of his past. He still sought to show the world that beauty he saw in it. Maybe it was why the memory prevailed.


### Sell Your Soul or Memory 2

All he wanted was for it to stop. He needed it to stop. 
No amount of talking, of assuring, of promising, that he would not talk of the Obitus Scholare ever was sufficient. He would not be able to, had he wanted to, his memories were gone, much like his sanity. The faint traces that remained did not make any sense. They were not coherent, not worth anything. Once he had known the names and faces of those before him. But even those memories had slowly but surely faded away. And yet, they were not convinced. 

He could not remember what the being had wanted in return for its help. He still was not sure what it took from him that night. What it had gained.
But it had freed him. The blood of his enemies painting the floor beneath his feet filled him with pure, unbridled ecstasy. The power it had granted him, flowing through his veins, giving him life was worth whatever price he might have paid.


### Body or Memory 3

The sensation of a body slowly malforming, bones creaking dangerously, skin tingling, something scratching, choking in the back of his throat, the coppery taste of blood, senses fading, returning, a horrid amalgam of painful experience. 
Every lie coming over his lips, scalding, hot and painful, made him contort further, his insides churning with something that wanted out, that should not be there in the first place. 

It had started with bleeding gums, teeth coming loose, then nails. Slowly, painfully sharper versions grew, pushing them out and aside in a bloodied mesh of meat and horn, skin and blood. Skin had slowly started to stretch, to break in parts, to give room for more eyes, for rough, leather-like growths to replace parts of its surface. Like a useless, thin layer of fabric, the elven skin had ripped apart, revealing gray carapace, jagged, sharp, unfeeling. 

The churning inside him had slowly died down, calmed, coiled, as if a living being. 
Until, finally, bones broke and regrew, flesh and muscle shifted aside, skin opened up to accommodate the arthropod appendages digging their way to the outside. 

The horror in his captors eyes told him all he needed to know back then. Today, the memory still brought a smile to Caligo’s lips. The fear of facing the wrath of whatever being had turned their hostage into a Scourged abomination burnt behind their horrified faces.
He should paint the memory while it remained, Caligo mused. Maybe he could capture the looks on their faces.


## NPCs

### Besian Starkweather

a hunter working relentlessly to rise in the ranks, loyal to the Radiant Church and all but blind in faith. A skilled fighter but driven by a relentless hunger for adoration and his personalized version of justice. The Scourged must be eradicated, especially those that pretend to ally with Luyarnha. The rules of the Radiant Church are to be followed at all times, no exception, no excuse. Anyone caught lacking shall pay for their blasphemy. Only the strongest and purest shall prevail. 

### Marcus Kellen

aka Shadow, a spy and smuggler for the forsaken, whose identity Caligo found out entirely by chance. He had been sent out to hunt near one of the former underground paths still leading into the Ruby District, somewhat accessibly (of course, two hunts later, after the Church found out about it, it was sealed), which Marcus had decided to use as a passageway that very night. A blessing in disguise, seeing as Caligo had turned out to be no devout follower of the Radiant Church at all, but rather in favor of the Forsaken, much to Marcus’ surprise.

### Elysia Cerys

Caligo’s sister, though the two have not spoken in many years. Their adult names, taken before the end of the war, a testament to their separation. The two joined the Obitus Scholare together, and she, unlike him, is still sworn to them. To her, the thing that returned from behind enemy lines, that beast that claimed to be her brother, was not someone she recognized. Maybe too late, she learned to look past his cursed appearance. Because when she realized her mistake, when she finally caught a glimpse of the brother she had grieved for so long, the Obitus Scholare had shattered every last piece of him that she so desperately wanted to save. 
Caligo Akrasia, walking in her brother’s stead is nothing like the person he once was. They had turned him into a bloodthirsty monster and she had let them. And yet, she could not look back. She could not waste any more time grieving family she had lost so long ago. She had to move on.
